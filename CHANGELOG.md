## 2026-11-14 — Two QAReady audiobooks (Andrew narration)

- **Layman:** Added two full audiobooks narrated by the natural Microsoft neural voice Andrew: *The QAReady Plan* (15 chapters — the 24-week study schedule, the seven phases, the portfolio and the career guide) and *ISTQB Foundation Level* (32 chapters — a complete CTFL v4.0 course covering all six syllabus chapters, worked examples, practice questions and exam strategy). Both also appear as EPUBs with read-along timings in the library.
- **Technical:** Added `source/qaready-plan.json` and `source/istqb-ctfl.json`, generated by `tools/make_qa_audiobooks.py` (which draws on QAReady's phases, weeks, career kit, study schedule and career guide). Built with `build.py`, producing per-chapter MP3s, combined `audiobook.mp3`, EPUB, `book.json` and a regenerated `books/catalog.json`.
- **Pipeline fix:** The free Edge TTS endpoint intermittently closed a websocket with "No audio was received" under bursty concurrency. `build.py` now uses a per-chapter `asyncio.Semaphore(4)` with a small stagger, and `synthesize()` retries up to 7 times with jittered backoff, removing partial files between attempts. Also upgraded the venv from edge-tts 7.2.8 (403 handshake) back to the documented working 7.3.1.
- **Also:** the two books are referenced from QAReady's new Audiobooks view, with local MP3 copies for offline playback.
